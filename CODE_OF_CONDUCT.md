# Code of Conduct

VTUHub is built by and for students. Everyone should feel welcome here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind (including caste, religion,
  gender, language, region or college).
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting pirated material: question-paper scans, textbook PDFs or copied notes.
- Spam, self-promotion or links to paid "leaked paper" sites.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem privately, email
**vtuhub2021@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
